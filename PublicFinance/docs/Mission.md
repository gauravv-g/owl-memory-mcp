# Mission Statement

## Our Mission

To democratize access to India's public finance data by transforming complex government financial information into clear, actionable insights that empower every citizen to understand, track, and engage with how their tax money is allocated and spent.

## What We Do

We aggregate fragmented public finance data from Union budgets, state budgets, ministry reports, and local government records. We apply AI-powered analysis, multilingual translation, and intuitive design to make this data accessible to anyone with a smartphone—regardless of their expertise, language, or internet connectivity.

## Why We Exist

Public money should be publicly understood. When citizens cannot access or comprehend government financial data, accountability suffers, corruption thrives, and democratic participation weakens. We exist to close this gap.

## How We Measure Success

- **Comprehension**: Can a farmer in rural Bihar understand how much funding their village received?
- **Coverage**: Is every rupee of public expenditure traceable on our platform?
- **Frequency**: Do citizens return weekly to track developments in their areas of interest?
- **Citation**: Are journalists, researchers, and policymakers using our data as the authoritative source?
- **Change**: Does increased transparency lead to better governance outcomes?

## Our Commitment

We commit to political neutrality, data accuracy, open access, and continuous improvement. We will never sell user data, never accept funding that compromises our independence, and never compromise on accessibility.
