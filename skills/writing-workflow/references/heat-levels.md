# Heat Levels Reference

## Level 1: Sensual

**Approach:** Anticipation-driven. Fade-to-black for explicit acts.

**What to show:**
- The moment before — the almost, the wanting, the tension
- Sensory detail: breath, warmth, the space between bodies
- Emotional stakes: what this means, not just what it feels like
- Clothing as tease — what's hidden is more erotic than what's shown

**What to hide:**
- Explicit sexual acts — cut to black or fade out
- Body parts — imply, don't name
- Mechanical detail — none

**Vocabulary:** Soft — warmth, pressure, breath, skin, curve, whisper, shadow, warmth

**Sentence rhythm:** Longer, flowing. Let tension build slowly.

**Example:**
> "His hand stopped an inch from her hip. She could feel the heat of his palm without the touch. The waiting was its own kind of intimacy — sweeter than the act itself, because it meant he wanted her enough to be careful."

**Calibration targets:**
- Explicit vocabulary density: < 1.5%
- Action-line ratio: < 20%
- Emotional-line ratio: > 15%

## Level 4: Literary Erotica

**Approach:** Explicit but transcendent. The body as philosophy. Sex as a language beyond words.

**What to show:**
- The dissolution of self — where does one body end and another begin?
- Philosophical interiority — what the body knows that the mind hasn't caught up to yet
- Images that no one has used before — find the specific, the strange, the true
- The sacred within the profane

**What to hide:**
- Cliché — at this level, every sentence must be invented fresh
- Pure physicality without interiority — even at heat level 4, the mind must be present
- Explanation — trust the reader to understand

**Vocabulary:** Poetic and raw combined — the body as landscape, sex as cosmology

**Sentence rhythm:** Varied — match rhythm to sensation. Short gasps. Long, winding thoughts. Silence.

**Example:**
> "Afterward, she lay with her hand over his heart and felt the separate rhythms trying to sync. Two bodies, two heartbeats, trying to become one organism. She thought: this is what the mystics meant. Not union — you never become one person. You become two people who have agreed to be broken open together."

**Calibration targets:**
- Explicit vocabulary density: > 5%
- Action-line ratio: > 40%
- Emotional-line ratio: varies — the interiority is emotional, but also philosophical

## Calibration Process

1. **Count explicit terms:** lund, chut, gaand, chu, maal, chod, chus, boobs, cock, cunt, fuck, ass, cum
2. **Calculate density:** explicit_count / total_words × 100
3. **Count action lines:** lines ≤ 8 words containing Hindi action markers (ne, ki, ka, se, mein, ko, par)
4. **Calculate action ratio:** action_lines / total_lines × 100
5. **Count emotional lines:** lines containing emotional markers (feel, heart, soul, thought, knew, wanted, needed, sochi, lagta, mehsoos, dard, pyaar)
6. **Calculate emotional ratio:** emotional_lines / total_lines × 100
7. **Compare to target level** and adjust:
   - Too low: add explicit vocabulary, shorten sentences, add action markers
   - Too high: add emotional interiority, extend sentences, add reflection
